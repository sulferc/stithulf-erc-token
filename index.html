What is Stithulf ERC Token?

Stithulf ERC Token (SULFERC) is the token of the Stithulf ecosystem. It was created on Ethereum Mainnet as an ERC-20 token, with a total supply at creation of 1,094,795,585 tokens.

The tokens were bridged to BNB Smart Chain (BSC) using the Wormhole bridge, and the wrapped tokens were paired with WBNB in a liquidity pool on PancakeSwap V3, where the token trades today.

Two contract addresses exist for one token: the original on Ethereum and the Wormhole-wrapped version on BSC. Always confirm which chain you are on. Addresses are in the next section.

Risks in plain language

Read this before you buy. You can lose all of the money you put in.

  • Price can fall to zero

    Crypto tokens are volatile. There is no guarantee of any value or return.

  • Very thin liquidity

    The pool holds well under 1 WBNB. Small trades move the price a lot, and selling may be difficult.

  • Concentrated ownership

    The project team holds about 85% of the supply on BSC. Sales by the team could push the price down sharply.

  • Early stage, no audit

    The token contract has not had an independent audit. Code can fail in unexpected ways.

  • Bridge risk

    If the Wormhole bridge is attacked or fails, the wrapped BSC token could lose its backing.

  • Price does not equal value

    With so little liquidity, quoted market prices and market caps are weak indicators of what the token could be sold for.

  • Regulatory risk

    Rules for crypto differ by country and are changing. Your local law may restrict ownership or trading.

  • Scams and fakes

    Copycat tokens and sites exist. Only use the addresses on this page.
