What is Stithulf ERC Token?

Stithulf ERC Token (SULFERC) is the native token of the Stithulf ecosystem. It was originally created on Ethereum Mainnet as an ERC-20 token, which is the most widely supported and audited token standard in the industry.

To reach lower fees and a larger retail trading base, the token was bridged to BNB Smart Chain (BSC) using the Wormhole bridge. The bridged tokens were then paired with WBNB in a liquidity pool on PancakeSwap V3, where the token trades today.

Two contract addresses exist for one token: the original on Ethereum and the Wormhole-wrapped version on BSC. Always confirm which chain you are on. Addresses are listed in the next section.

Token journey

From issuance to trading, in four verifiable steps.

  1. 1

    Issued on Ethereum

    Created as an ERC-20 token on Ethereum Mainnet. Contract address listed above.

  2. 2

    Bridged with Wormhole

    Tokens were locked on Ethereum and equivalent wrapped tokens were issued on BNB Smart Chain.

  3. 3

    Paired with WBNB

    The wrapped tokens were deposited with WBNB into a PancakeSwap V3 liquidity pool.

  4. 4

    Open trading

    Anyone with a BSC wallet can trade SULFERC against WBNB through the pool.

Liquidity and how to buy

SULFERC trades on BNB Smart Chain in a PancakeSwap V3 pool against WBNB.

Trade on PancakeSwap View V3 pool

  1. 1

    Set up a BSC wallet

    Any wallet that supports BNB Smart Chain works. Fund it with BNB for the trade and for network fees.

  2. 2

    Open PancakeSwap

    Use the button above or go to pancakeswap.finance and select the BNB Smart Chain network.

  3. 3

    Verify the BSC address

    Paste the BSC contract address from this page. Do not search by name, since copycat tokens exist.

  4. 4

    Review and swap

    Check the price impact and slippage shown, confirm in your wallet, then verify the transaction on BscScan.

Liquidity depth changes constantly. Check the pool page for current liquidity before trading, and consider splitting large orders to limit price impact.

Contract and liquidity controls

  • Source code verified on Etherscan and BscScan.
  • No mint function. Supply is fixed.
  • Ownership renounced. No admin can change token rules.
  • Liquidity position locked for 24 months.
  • Treasury is a multisig requiring 3 of 5 signers.
  • Bridge risk disclosed. The BSC token depends on Wormhole infrastructure.

Risks in plain language

Read this before you buy. You can lose all of the money you put in.

  • Price can fall to zero

    Crypto tokens are volatile. There is no guarantee of any value or return.

  • Thin liquidity

    A small pool means large trades move the price a lot, and selling may be hard.

  • Bridge risk

    If the Wormhole bridge is attacked or fails, the wrapped BSC token could lose its backing.

  • Smart contract risk

    Audits find many bugs but not all. Code can fail in unexpected ways.

  • Concentration

    A few wallets hold most of the supply. Their actions can affect the market.

  • Unlock events

    When vested tokens unlock, more supply can reach the market and push the price down.

  • Regulatory risk

    Rules for crypto differ by country and are changing. Your local law may restrict ownership or trading.

  • Scams and fakes

    Copycat tokens and sites exist. Only use the addresses on this page.
